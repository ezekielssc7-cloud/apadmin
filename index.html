    Privacy and your data

    How APAdmin handles your personal data, in line with Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA).

    What we collect

    • Your TP number and first name, read from your student card.
    • Your tickets: the service, times, and whether you were seen.
    • Documents you send, your note, and the office's reply.
    • Your email address, if you add one for alerts.

    What we don't keep

    • The photo of your card. It's only used to read your TP number, then discarded.
    • Anything you type to the AI assistant is not linked to your student record.

    Why we use it

    To run the queue, call you to the right counter, handle your request, and send the alerts you chose. We don't use it for marketing.

    Who can see it

    Staff at the Student Admin Office team handling your request. Documents go only to the team you choose.

    How long we keep it

    • Documents: deleted 90 days after your request is closed.
    • Queue history: kept for one semester for service statistics, then deleted.

    Your rights

    You can ask to see the data we hold about you, correct it, or withdraw consent for future requests. Ask at General Enquiries (Counter 4) or through the AI assistant.

    Security

    Data travels over encrypted connections, uploads are checked for viruses, and only staff accounts can open your documents.

    This is a sample notice for the prototype. The university's data protection officer should approve the final wording.
